PromptVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

PromptVault is a local-first Android app for saving and running AI prompt templates. This policy describes how the app handles information.

Data storage

Prompts and app preferences are stored only on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your prompt content for version 1 of the app.

Data collection

We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data. There is no account system in version 1.

Analytics and third parties

Version 1 does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. If that changes in a future release, this policy will be updated before those features ship.

Permissions

The app uses storage on your device for the prompt library. Network access is used only when you open links (such as this policy) in your browser—not to upload your prompts.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact option on the Google Play store listing for PromptVault.
